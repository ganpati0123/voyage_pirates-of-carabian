Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before setting sail. Still curious? Reach out to our crew.

Who can participate?+

Anyone with a passion for building — students, professionals, self-taught coders, designers, and dreamers. All skill levels welcome. Whether you're a seasoned captain or stepping on deck for the first time, there's a place for you aboard Voyage Hackathon.

Do I need a team?+

Teams can be 2-4 members. If you're sailing solo, don't worry — we have a crew-matching board to help you find teammates before the voyage begins.

Is there a registration fee?+

No! Voyage Hackathon is completely free to participate. Just bring your laptop, your creativity, and your sense of adventure. Food and swag are on us.

Can I participate online?+

Yes! Voyage Hackathon supports both onsite and online participation. Remote pirates get the same access to mentors, resources, and prizes as those on the island.

What should I bring?+

Bring your laptop, charger, any hardware you want to hack on, and a reusable water bottle. We provide food, caffeine, swag, sleeping bags, and high-speed internet.

How are projects judged?+

Projects are evaluated on four criteria: innovation (30%), technical complexity (25%), design & UX (20%), and presentation (25%). Judges include industry leaders, tech veterans, and past hackathon winners.

Can I use existing code?+

You can use open-source libraries, APIs, and frameworks. However, your project's core logic and implementation must be built during the 36-hour hackathon window. No pre-built projects.
